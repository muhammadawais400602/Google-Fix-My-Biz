Why it happened

Six reasons Google suspends a profile.

Suspensions are rarely random. In our 1,200+ recoveries, nearly all trace back to one of these six triggers, and many profiles have two.

01
📮

Virtual office or PO box address

Google requires a real, staffed location. Mailbox stores, coworking desks and registered-agent addresses are the most common trigger we see.

02
🔤

Keyword-stuffed business name

"Best Austin Plumber 24/7 Cheap" is not your legal name. The name must match your real-world signage, not a search phrase.

03
🏷️

Ineligible category or business type

Some models, such as lead-gen sites, pure online stores and rented-out service areas without a real presence, do not qualify for a profile.

04
🧩

Address mismatch across directories

When your website, Yelp and Facebook disagree about suite numbers or phone numbers, Google's trust signals break down.

05
👯

Duplicate listings

Two profiles for one location, often left behind by an old agency or a previous owner, can cause both to be flagged.

06
🚧

Review gating or incentivized reviews

Only asking happy customers, or offering discounts for five stars, violates policy and can trigger a suspension wave.

Know which one you have

Hard suspension vs soft suspension.

The fix is different, and so is the timeline. We confirm which you have in the first audit.

Hard suspension

The listing is gone from Maps

What you see
"Suspended" banner in the dashboard. Customers cannot find you on Maps or Search.
Why
A serious or repeated guideline violation, such as an ineligible address or fake details.
Fix
A formal reinstatement appeal with an evidence pack. Typical turnaround is 72 hours.
Soft suspension

The listing shows, but you can't edit

What you see
The profile appears but you cannot manage it, or edits are blocked and unverified.
Why
Verification lapsed, or a flag from suspicious edits. It often clears faster.
Fix
Correct the flagged data and re-verify. Often resolved within one to two days.

Suspension recovery questions

How do I know if my profile is suspended?

You will see a "Suspended" banner in your Business Profile dashboard, the listing disappears from Maps, or you can no longer edit it. Our free audit can confirm which type you have.

How long does reinstatement really take?

Our typical time is 72 hours. Simple soft suspensions can clear in a day or two. Complex hard suspensions, such as multiple triggers, can take longer, and we tell you that upfront.

Can you guarantee reinstatement?

No one can, because Google makes the decision. Our reinstatement rate is 94%, and if we cannot get you reinstated we will tell you honestly after the audit before you spend more.

What does it cost?

Suspension recovery is $499 one-time, including the audit, evidence pack, appeal and follow-ups. Citation cleanup is $2 per listing if you want it added.

Will you need my Google password?

Never. You invite us as a manager on the profile, or we work from screenshots and documents you send. You stay the owner.

What if my business has no storefront?

Service-area businesses can qualify when they hide the home address and show service areas. We review your setup and tell you honestly if you do not qualify.

What happens to my reviews and rankings?

On reinstatement, reviews usually return with the profile. Rankings often recover over one to three weeks as the profile re-establishes trust, and we monitor them.

What does the 90-day warranty cover?

If the profile is suspended again for the same underlying issue within 90 days of reinstatement, we re-file the appeal at no charge. New unrelated violations are not covered.
