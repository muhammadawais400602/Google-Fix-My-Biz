Northline Plumbing: from rank 14 to the top 3 in Denver

Challenge

Northline's profile was suspended after a Google review flagged its service-area address. Emergency calls dropped to near zero overnight, and once the profile returned, they sat at an average rank of 14 across the Denver metro.

What we did

We diagnosed the policy trigger, filed a documented appeal with proof of business, converted the listing to a service-area business, rebuilt 120 citations by hand and started a review-request flow for completed jobs.

Result

Reinstated in 48 hours. Within 10 weeks the average rank went from 14.2 to 2.6 and inbound calls rose 186%. Use the map to flip between the first week and week ten.
