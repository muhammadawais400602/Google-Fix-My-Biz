Billing questions

The fine print, in plain English

Is the $499 recovery fee really one-time?

Yes. Recovery is a fixed fee covering diagnosis, the appeal and any follow-up with Google until the case closes. There is no retainer and no hourly billing.

How does annual billing work?

Annual billing takes 15% off the Managed Starter and Managed Growth monthly price and is charged once up front. Starter becomes about $424 per month and Growth about $849 per month.

Can I switch plans later?

Any time. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades apply at the next billing date.

What happens if you cannot reinstate my profile?

You are not charged the recovery fee. We will tell you honestly if a case looks unwinnable before we start, and walk you through the alternatives, such as a fresh verified listing.

Are citations billed separately?

Citations are $2 each with a 50 citation minimum. Managed plans include citations in the monthly price, so you only pay a la carte if you are not on a plan.

Do you offer agency or franchise discounts?

Yes. The Scale plan is priced by volume, with white-label reporting and API access. Contact us for a quote within one business day.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards, and ACH or invoice billing for annual and Scale plans. See the terms for refund details, or browse the full FAQ.
