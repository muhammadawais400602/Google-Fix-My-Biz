googlefixmybiz ("we", "us") helps businesses recover and grow their Google Business Profiles. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit googlefixmybiz.co, use our dashboard or work with our team. It is a general template and not legal advice.

1. What we collect

  • Details you give us: name, email, phone number, business name and address, and anything you write in a form or message.
  • Case materials: documents and photos you share for a suspension case, such as licenses, utility bills and storefront images.
  • Account and billing data: plan, invoices and the last four digits of your card. Card numbers are handled by our payment processor, not stored by us.
  • Usage data: pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location and referring site.
  • Business profile data: information visible on your Google Business Profile, plus access you grant us as a profile manager.

2. How we use it

  • To deliver the services you request, including audits, recovery cases and ranking reports.
  • To reply to your messages, usually within 4 business hours.
  • To process payments and send invoices.
  • To send product updates and our newsletter, only if you opt in. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To improve our site, tools and training, and to prevent fraud and abuse.

3. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies for essential site function, to remember preferences and to understand aggregate traffic. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features, such as the dashboard login, will not work without essential cookies.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the business, such as hosting, email, payments and analytics, under contracts that limit their use of your data.
  • Google, to the extent needed to submit and manage your reinstatement or profile changes on your behalf.
  • Authorities or advisors when required by law or to protect our rights.
  • A successor if we are involved in a merger or sale of the company.

5. Retention

We keep account and billing records for as long as your account is active and for up to seven years afterward for tax and accounting. Case documents are deleted within 12 months after a case closes, or sooner on request. Marketing contacts are kept until you unsubscribe.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including Texas, California and Canada), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to certain uses and to withdraw consent. To exercise a right, email us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days and may need to verify your identity first.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and least-privilege permissions for staff. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password for your account and never share it with us over email.

Not affiliated with Google. googlefixmybiz is an independent company and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google LLC.
