If your profile is suspended, start with recovery, because nothing else matters until it is live. Otherwise start with the free audit. It tells you whether ranking, reviews or citations is your biggest gap.

Yes. Suspension recovery is $499 one-time and citations are $2 each. Managed local SEO is the ongoing plan, from $499 a month.

No one honestly can. We guarantee the work: a documented plan, monthly grid scans so you can see movement, and for suspensions a 90-day reinstatement warranty.

Do I need to give you access to my Google account?

No passwords. You add us as a manager on your Business Profile, which you can remove at any time.
