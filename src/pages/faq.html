We recover suspended Google Business Profiles, rebuild local map-pack rankings, clean up citations and manage reviews. Since 2019 our team of 40+ specialists has recovered 1,200+ profiles for local businesses, agencies and franchises.

Run the free audit. It checks your profile status, categories, citations and local rankings, then tells you in plain English what to fix first. If you would rather talk it through, book a 20-minute demo.

Which businesses do you work with?

Single-location shops, service-area businesses (plumbers, electricians, movers), multi-location brands and agencies managing client profiles. We cover the US and Canada, with our offices in Austin and Toronto.

How long before I see results?

Suspension recovery is typically resolved in about 72 hours once we submit a complete case. Citation cleanups show movement in 3 to 6 weeks, and map-pack ranking gains usually build over 60 to 90 days.

Do I need to give you my Google password?

Never. We ask you to add us as a manager on your Business Profile, which you can revoke at any time. We will never ask for your password or two-step verification codes.

Why was my Google Business Profile suspended?

The most common triggers are a mismatch between your name or address and what Google can verify, a virtual or shared address, keyword-stuffed business names, unapproved edits, and categories that do not match your real services. Sometimes it is an automated flag with no real violation.

How does suspension recovery work?

We audit what triggered the flag, gather the documents Google expects (licenses, utility bills, signage photos, a short walkthrough video where needed), fix profile issues and file a complete reinstatement request. We then follow the case until it is resolved. See the full recovery process.

How often do you win a reinstatement?

Our reinstatement rate is 94%. The typical reinstatement lands in 72 hours. If we cannot get your profile back, see the refund question under Billing.

Can I just create a new profile instead?

Please do not. A duplicate profile at the same address can trigger a second suspension and often makes the original harder to recover. Reinstating the original keeps your reviews, photos and history.

What is the difference between a soft and a hard suspension?

A soft suspension leaves your profile visible but locks edits and removes owner access. A hard suspension removes the listing from Search and Maps entirely. Both are recoverable, but hard suspensions need a full reinstatement case.

What if my profile gets suspended again after reinstatement?

Every recovery includes our 90-day reinstatement warranty. If the same issue gets your profile suspended again within 90 days, we re-file at no extra cost.

Do you ever use fake addresses or paperwork?

No, and we will turn down cases that need it. We only do white-hat work. If your real address cannot be verified, we help you find a compliant path, such as converting to a service-area business.

What is the map pack and why does it matter?

The map pack is the block of three local results with a map at the top of many Google searches. Those three spots capture most clicks and calls for local queries, so ranking there is usually worth more than ranking number one in organic results.

What factors decide my local ranking?

Google weighs relevance, distance and prominence. In practice that means accurate categories and services, consistent name, address and phone data across the web, review quantity and quality, and engagement signals. Read the 2026 ranking factors guide.

What are citations and do I still need them?

Citations are listings of your name, address and phone number on directories and data aggregators. Inconsistent ones confuse Google about who you are. We clean up duplicates and fix errors at $2 per citation. The citation playbook explains the details.

Why do I rank well near my shop but disappear a mile away?

Local rankings depend on the searcher's location. That is why we show results as a heat map grid rather than a single number. Our tracking grid reveals the gaps so we can target those neighborhoods.

Can you guarantee a number one ranking?

No one honestly can. We guarantee the work and the process, and we report your actual position every month. What we can show you is the before-and-after track record in our case studies.

Can you remove a fake or malicious review?

If it violates Google's review policies (spam, conflict of interest, off-topic, harassment), we can flag it with evidence and escalate. Genuine but unflattering reviews cannot be removed, and we will tell you that upfront. See the fake review removal guide.

How can I get more reviews the right way?

Ask every happy customer, make it a single tap with your review link or QR code, and reply to every review. We never buy, trade or fabricate reviews, because that gets profiles suspended.

Should I reply to negative reviews?

Yes, briefly and calmly, within a couple of days. Acknowledge the issue, offer to resolve it offline and never argue. Prospective customers read your replies more closely than the complaint itself.

Why did my reviews disappear?

Google filters reviews it suspects are spam, and a suspension can hide them temporarily. We check whether they were filtered or removed, and recovering the profile often restores them.
