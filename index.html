FAQ

What does the suspension recovery process look like?+
We start with a free audit to identify the violation. If recoverable, we file the reinstatement with full documentation — business license, utility bills, photos, signage — then follow up with Google every 48 hours until resolved. Most reinstatements land within 72 hours. Every step shows in your dashboard.
What services does Google Fix My Biz provide?+
Everything inside Google Business Profile: suspension recovery, NAP and citation cleanup across 300+ directories, review management, map-pack optimization, category and service tuning, posts, Q&A seeding, photo and video optimization, insights reporting, and local SEO strategy. We do not do general websites, Google Ads, or social media.
How long does it take to see map-pack ranking improvements?+
Most clients see rank movement within 30 days, and top-three placement within 60–90 days for moderately competitive categories. Harder niches (lawyers, dentists in major metros) take 3–6 months. We send a city-wide rank grid every two weeks so you see progress in real coordinates.
Do you work with multi-location brands and franchises?+
Yes. We've rolled out and manage profiles for franchises with up to 80 locations. Multi-location clients get a bulk-management dashboard, centralized review handling, and consistent brand voice across every listing, plus franchisee-level reporting.
Can you remove fake or bad-faith reviews?+
We can flag any review that violates Google's content policies — fake, off-topic, from competitors, spam. We can't remove legitimate negative reviews (nobody can), but we'll draft public responses that preserve your reputation and, where appropriate, reach out directly to resolve the issue.
What if my profile gets suspended again later?+
Active clients are covered — if a managed profile gets suspended, we reinstate it at no extra cost. One-off recovery clients get a 90-day warranty: same-reason recurrences within 90 days get free follow-up filings.
Do you offer fixed-price work or monthly retainers?+
Both. Suspension recovery and audits are fixed-price. Ongoing optimization and multi-location work are monthly retainers scaled to profile count and competitive intensity. Both quotes come after the free audit.
What businesses are you not a fit for?+
We don't take on Google-restricted categories — adult, gambling, unlicensed medical, counterfeit goods, high-risk financial. We also decline clients looking for black-hat tactics like fake reviews or keyword-stuffed names. Everything we do stays inside Google's guidelines, which is why reinstatements last.
