(06) FAQ

Answers to the honest questions every owner asks.

What does the suspension recovery process look like?+
We start with a free profile audit to identify the likely violation. If it's recoverable, we file the reinstatement request with full supporting documentation (business license, utility bills, photos, signage), then follow up with Google every 48 hours until it's resolved. Most reinstatements land within 72 hours. You'll see every step inside your client dashboard.
What services does Google Fix My Biz provide?+
Everything inside Google Business Profile: suspension recovery, reinstatements, NAP and citation cleanup across 300+ directories, review management (responses, flagging, drip programs), map-pack optimization, category and service tuning, posts, Q&A seeding, photo and video optimization, insights reporting, and local SEO strategy. We do not do general websites, Google Ads, or social media.
How long does it take to see map-pack ranking improvements?+
Most clients see meaningful rank movement within 30 days of the initial optimization, and top-three placement within 60–90 days for moderately competitive categories. Harder niches (lawyers, dentists in major metros) typically take 3–6 months. We send you a city-wide rank grid every two weeks so you can see progress in real coordinates, not vague "trending up" language.
Do you work with multi-location brands and franchises?+
Yes. We've rolled out and manage profiles for franchises with up to 80 locations. Multi-location clients get a bulk-management dashboard, centralized review handling, and consistent brand voice across every listing — plus franchisee-level reporting if needed.
Can you remove fake or bad-faith reviews?+
We can flag any review that violates Google's content policies (fake, off-topic, from competitors, spam). We can't remove legitimate negative reviews — nobody can — but we'll draft public responses that preserve your reputation and, where appropriate, reach out to the reviewer directly to resolve the underlying issue so they update or remove it themselves.
What if my profile gets suspended again later?+
All active clients are covered — if a profile we manage gets suspended during the engagement, we reinstate it at no extra cost. For one-off recovery clients, we include a 90-day warranty: if the same suspension reason recurs within 90 days, the follow-up filing is free.
Do you offer fixed-price work or monthly retainers?+
Both. Suspension recovery and audits are fixed-price, so you know the cost before you start. Ongoing optimization and multi-location work are monthly retainers scaled to the number of profiles and the competitive intensity of your markets. We'll quote both options after the free audit.
What businesses are you not a fit for?+
We don't take on businesses in categories Google restricts (adult, gambling, unlicensed medical, counterfeit goods, high-risk financial). We also decline clients looking for black-hat tactics — fake reviews, keyword-stuffed business names, fake addresses. Everything we do is within Google's published guidelines, which is why reinstatements last.
